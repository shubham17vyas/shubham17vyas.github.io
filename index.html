Over the past eight+ years, I’ve worked across software engineering, system integration, product delivery, and technical leadership. At C Squared Systems, I built reusable device integrations that cut code redundancy by roughly 20% and improved backend performance. I ask the right questions, build maintainable solutions, support the people around me, and stay accountable for the outcome. If you need someone who can understand the problem, work with the team, make decisions, and actually get things shipped, that’s where I do my best work.
